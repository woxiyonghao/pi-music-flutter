# PhonoraMusic Privacy Policy / 隐私政策

_Last updated / 最后更新: 2026-11-23_

## English

PhonoraMusic ("the App") is a local high-fidelity music player. **We do not collect, store, sell or share any personal data on our servers.** The App has no accounts, no advertising and no analytics or crash-reporting SDKs.

**What the App accesses on your device**
- **Audio files and CUE sheets** in your storage, only to build your music library and play it. File names, tags and cover art are stored in a local database on your device and never uploaded.
- **Storage permissions** (`READ_MEDIA_AUDIO`, `READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE`, and "All files access") are used solely to scan and read your music and its CUE sidecar files.
- **Notifications** (`POST_NOTIFICATIONS`) and **foreground service / wake lock** are used to keep playback running with the screen off and to show the playback notification.
- **Network (`INTERNET`)** is used only for optional local-network features that you turn on yourself, such as the Wi-Fi file transfer page and the web remote control, which are served from your own device to other devices on your local network. No data is sent to us or to third parties.

**Data sharing:** none. **Data retention:** all data stays on your device and is removed when you uninstall the App.

**Children:** the App does not knowingly collect data from anyone, including children.

**Changes:** if this policy changes, the updated version will be published at this page with a new date.

## 中文

PhonoraMusic(以下简称"本应用")是一款本地高保真音乐播放器。**我们不会在服务器上收集、存储、出售或共享任何个人数据。** 本应用没有账号系统,没有广告,也没有任何统计分析或崩溃上报 SDK。

**本应用在你的设备上访问的内容**
- **存储中的音频文件和 CUE 文件**:仅用于建立曲库和播放。文件名、标签、封面保存在设备本地数据库中,不会上传。
- **存储权限**(`READ_MEDIA_AUDIO`、`READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE` 以及"所有文件访问权限"):仅用于扫描和读取你的音乐及其 CUE 附属文件。
- **通知权限**(`POST_NOTIFICATIONS`)与**前台服务/唤醒锁**:用于息屏后持续播放,并显示播放通知。
- **网络权限(`INTERNET`)**:仅用于你主动开启的局域网功能,例如 Wi-Fi 传歌页面和网页遥控。这些功能由你的设备向局域网内其他设备提供服务,不会向我们或第三方发送任何数据。

**数据共享**:无。**数据保留**:所有数据都保存在你的设备上,卸载应用后即被删除。

**儿童**:本应用不会收集任何人(包括儿童)的数据。

**政策变更**:如本政策有更新,会在本页面发布并更新日期。

**联系方式**:YOUR_EMAIL@example.com
